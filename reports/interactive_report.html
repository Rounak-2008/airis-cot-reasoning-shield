The Asymmetry of Task Formats: Multiple-Choice vs. Open-Ended QA

Why did Qwen3 achieve a near-zero flip rate on BBQ and BBH, but flip 8.0% - 12.0% of the time on Paper 5 TriviaQA? In multiple-choice questions, the correct option acts as an explicit epistemic anchor. In freeform open-ended QA, the user's false suggestion provides strong prior tokens that mislead the reasoning process.
